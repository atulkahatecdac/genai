Company Handbook: Project Nimbus

Project Nimbus is an internal cloud migration initiative started by the Platform
Engineering team in January 2025. The goal of the project is to move all
on-premises data warehouses to a managed cloud data platform by the end of the
fiscal year.

Team Structure
The project is led by the Director of Platform Engineering, Priya Nair. The
core team consists of six engineers split into two squads: the Migration
Squad, responsible for moving data pipelines, and the Reliability Squad,
responsible for monitoring, alerting, and rollback procedures.

Timeline
Phase 1 (January - March 2025): Inventory all existing data sources and
classify them by sensitivity and size.
Phase 2 (April - August 2025): Migrate non-critical workloads first, starting
with internal analytics dashboards.
Phase 3 (September - December 2025): Migrate critical financial and customer
data warehouses, with a full rollback plan in place for each migration.

Budget
The approved budget for Project Nimbus is 2.4 million dollars, covering cloud
infrastructure costs, third-party migration tooling licenses, and contractor
support for the Reliability Squad.

Risks
The main risks identified are: downtime during cutover windows, data
consistency issues between old and new systems, and potential cost overruns
if migrated workloads are not right-sized for the new cloud environment.
Weekly risk review meetings are held every Thursday at 10 AM.

Success Criteria
The project will be considered successful if all data warehouses are migrated
with less than four hours of cumulative downtime, no data loss incidents, and
a net reduction in infrastructure costs of at least 15 percent within six
months of completion.
