Product Onboarding Notes

New users are guided through a 3-step setup wizard: connect a data source,
choose a workspace template, and invite at least one teammate. Users who
complete all 3 steps in their first session are roughly twice as likely to
still be active after 30 days.

The most common drop-off point is the data source connection step, largely
because users are asked to grant broad permissions before understanding why
they're needed. Adding a short explainer before the permissions prompt is
planned for the next release.

Support tickets tagged "onboarding" made up 18% of all tickets last quarter,
down from 27% the quarter before, after the setup wizard redesign shipped.
